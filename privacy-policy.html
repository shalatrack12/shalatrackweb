Privacy Policy

SHALATRACK School ERP System

Effective Date: 24 July 2026

SHALATRACK ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy of schools, administrators, teachers, students, and all users of our School ERP platform.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information while using SHALATRACK:

  • School Information
  • Administrator Details
  • Teacher Details
  • Student Details
  • Attendance Records
  • Email Addresses
  • Authentication Information

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Authenticate users securely.
  • Manage school administration.
  • Record and maintain attendance.
  • Improve platform performance.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Maintain platform security.

3. Firebase Services

SHALATRACK uses Google Firebase services including:

  • Firebase Authentication
  • Cloud Firestore Database

These services securely process and store application data.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, modification, or disclosure.

5. Data Sharing

SHALATRACK does not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. Data is used solely to provide school management services.

6. Children's Privacy

Student information is managed only by authorized school administrators and teachers.

7. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page.

8. Contact Us

Email: support@shalatrack.in
